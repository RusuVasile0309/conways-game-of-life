## What Went Well

**Highest AI accuracy of the entire build.** Pure functions, no framework entanglement, well-specified domain — AI generated correct implementations on the first pass for stories 2.1, 2.2, and 2.4. The test scaffolding matched the behavior-first intent of the brief without padding.

**Injectable RNG pattern nailed in one pass.** `randomizeGrid(grid, density = 0.3, rng = Math.random): Grid` with a `mulberry32` seeded PRNG for tests — correct signature, correct test strategy, no correction needed. This is the kind of deterministic-domain work where AI adds maximum speed.

**AI caught an AC error and wrote the correct test anyway (story 2.3).** The epics.md AC for the 3×3 all-alive case stated "the four corner cells die" — which contradicts Conway's rule 3 (cells with exactly 3 neighbors survive). AI generated the correct assertion (corners survive) and added a comment in the test calling out the AC discrepancy explicitly. This was a notable demonstration of AI judgment over blind AC-following.

**Module boundary held throughout.** Not a single `import` from React, DOM, or any framework appeared in `libs/sim`. The boundary gate from Epic 1 paid off immediately.

---

## Challenges

**Story 2.2: Missing TypeScript project reference (`sim → types`).** AI implemented `step()` correctly but omitted the `references` entry in `tsconfig.json` that connects `libs/sim` to `libs/types`. `tsc` caught it in CI. One-line fix. Low cost but a pattern worth watching: AI tends to focus on the implementation and overlook build-graph wiring.

**No BMAD story files for Epic 2.** Planning happened at the epic level without structured story files (story files were introduced from Epic 4 onwards). AI worked from the epic description and verbal direction — effective for this domain, but the absence of explicit ACs as a dev contract meant less structure.

---

## Key Insights

1. **Deterministic, pure-function domains maximize AI value.** When the domain is well-specified and has no framework side effects, AI produces high-quality output with minimal correction. Epic 2 is the clearest evidence.

2. **AI will follow the wrong AC if not caught.** Story 2.3 worked out because AI happened to reason from first principles rather than blindly following the epics.md AC. In other contexts, AI would have followed the wrong spec. Explicit story files with validated ACs would reduce this risk.

3. **Build-graph wiring is a recurring AI blind spot.** Missing project references, missing `baseUrl` (Epic 1 story 1.2) — AI focuses on the code and skips the graph wiring. Worth checking tsconfig `references` after any new inter-lib imports.
